# London account/corporate & minicab/executive operators recruiting owner-drivers (own PHV car) — fit for a 2023 Skoda Superb Estate iV (PHEV), as of Nov 2026

Research method note: research date 2026-11-24. Direct page fetches to addisonlee.com and veezu.co.uk were **blocked in this research session** (host not allowed on the session network), so Addison Lee and Veezu facts below come from search-engine snippets of their own pages rather than full-page reads. Many operator pages carry no visible date. Treat everything not explicitly dated 2025–2026 as "check before relying on it". No operator page found in this research explicitly names the Skoda Superb / Superb Estate iV as accepted or rejected, except by its absence from published model lists.

## Q1. Addison Lee (incl. Green Tomato Cars & ComCab, which it owns): owner-driver scheme, vehicles, fees, insurance, work type

### Takeaway
Addison Lee runs an "already own a car" owner-driver route (car under 5 years old, black or dark grey, own hire & reward insurance, self-employed), but its published accepted-model list is a premium list (Audi A6/A8, BMW 5/7, Mercedes E/S-Class, Tesla, VW ID.4/Multivan) that, per the snippets found, does **not** include the Skoda Superb. The Superb Estate iV is therefore likely to be refused unless Addison Lee confirms otherwise. Addison Lee also owns Green Tomato Cars and ComCab.

### Cited Findings
- Owner-driver requirements published on Addison Lee's "already own a car" page: PCO licence; "Vehicle in good condition & no older than 5 years"; "Vehicle colour is black, or dark grey"; a list of accepted models. — [Addison Lee – Become a PCO Driver & Chauffeur with Own Car](https://www.addisonlee.com/do-you-already-own-a-car/)
- Accepted models listed (per search snippet of the same page): Audi A6, Audi A8, BMW 5 Series, BMW 7 Series, Mercedes-Benz E-Class and S-Class, Tesla (all models), VW ID.4, VW Multivan. Skoda Superb, Toyota Corolla and Prius were not named in any snippet. — [Addison Lee – already own a car](https://www.addisonlee.com/do-you-already-own-a-car/); [alt URL](https://addisonlee.com/already-own-a-car-5)
- Addison Lee claims owner-drivers "on average earn at least £1,500 p/w driving their own car" (company marketing claim, unverified). — [Addison Lee – already own a car](https://www.addisonlee.com/do-you-already-own-a-car/)
- The Addison Lee Driver app listing says "If you do own your own car, we accept high quality cars under 5 years old". — [App Store – Addison Lee Driver](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/addison-lee-driver/id6444405606)
- Own-car drivers need hire and reward insurance (not standard car insurance); all drivers must register as self-employed with HMRC or use a limited company. — [Addison Lee – How to register your car for private hire](https://www.addisonlee.com/addlib/how-to-register-your-car-for-private-hire/); [Addison Lee – Private Hire Drivers](https://www.addisonlee.com/private-hire-drivers/)
- A PCO licence is not restricted to one operator, so drivers can work for Addison Lee and ride-hailing apps / other operators. — [Addison Lee FAQs – drive with us](https://www.addisonlee.com/support/faqs/#drive-with-us)
- Flexible hours: "Addison Lee driver jobs are completely flexible. Work the hours that suit you best... specific incentives are in place to encourage drivers to work" at busy times. — [Addison Lee FAQ – work set shifts](https://addisonlee.com/support/faqs/drive-with-us/work-set-shifts)
- Addison Lee claims over 4,500 jobs a day in London. — [Addison Lee – become a driver](https://www.addisonlee.com/become-an-addison-lee-driver)
- Fees/commission: no current owner-driver weekly fee or commission % was found. Rental drivers pay £15–£165/week for EVs depending on a points target, and pay nothing if the weekly points target is met; new drivers get free hire for first 8 weeks (rental route, not owner route). — [Addison Lee – launches driver offer](https://www.addisonlee.com/addlib/addison-lee-launches-market-leading-driver-offer/); [Addison Lee – circuit expectations](https://addisonlee.com/addlib/what-is-it-like-to-work-the-addison-lee-circuit)
- Historic (2019, outdated) commission: 35% of fares plus £210/week rental, per IWGB strike coverage. — [Business & Human Rights Resource Centre (Guardian)](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/uk-addison-lee-drivers-strike-over-poor-wages-high-commission-rates)
- 2026: an employment tribunal backed Addison Lee drivers' claims on pay and working time, confirming drivers were paid on a weekly basis. — [Leigh Day press release 2026](https://www.leighday.co.uk/news/press-releases/2026-news/significant-victory-for-addison-lee-drivers-as-tribunal-backs-claims-on-pay-and-working-time/)
- Forum (older, anecdotal): owner-driver Mercedes scheme applicants were told cars ~3 years old were wanted; a blue E-Class was refused (black/grey/silver wanted). — [UberPeople thread](https://www.uberpeople.net/threads/addison-lee-owner-driver-scheme-for-mercedes-drivers.96614/)
- Driver reviews (Indeed, 2024–2025, small sample): mixed to negative on pay and hours ("very long hours", "long hours and bad pay"), London market described as saturated; some positives on weekly pay and job tracking. — [Indeed – Addison Lee reviews](https://uk.indeed.com/cmp/Addison-Lee-7/reviews)
- Addison Lee acquired Travelhire (Green Tomato Cars, 500+ vehicles) in July 2023. — [Wikipedia – Green Tomato Cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Tomato_Cars)
- Addison Lee acquired ComCab (Computer Cab plc), City Fleet Networks and Flightlink from ComfortDelGro (2021). ComCab is a black-cab dispatcher. — [Addison Lee – acquire ComCab](https://addisonlee.com/addlib/addison-lee-acquire-comcab)

### Inferences
- The Superb Estate iV sits below Addison Lee's published owner-driver model tier (executive German saloons/Tesla), so acceptance is unlikely; but Addison Lee also has an "executive/large" estate need, so a direct enquiry is the only way to be sure.
- Addison Lee's work is largely account/corporate and airport, dispatched through its own driver app. No evidence was found on whether owner-drivers can view and accept pre-booked jobs in advance (job board).

### Gaps
- Owner-driver weekly circuit fee / commission % in 2025–2026 not found (Addison Lee site could not be fetched; only snippets).
- Whether Skoda Superb / Superb Estate / PHEV is on the current accepted list: not confirmed either way (absent from snippets).
- Whether owner-drivers can use Addison Lee's own insurance vs their own annual H&R: own H&R is stated as required; no detail on accepting an existing annual policy's terms (e.g., minimum public liability).
- Pre-booked job visibility in the Addison Lee driver app: not found.

## Q2. Green Tomato Cars

### Takeaway
Green Tomato Cars (eco/hybrid account fleet, now owned by Addison Lee) historically took "owner drivers" with their own car, but the only source is a 2022 third-party guide; no current official own-car page was found.

### Cited Findings
- To drive with your own family car or a subscription car, you register as an "Owner driver"; documents include valid MOT, logbook, insurance, proof of address, NI number; smart dress code (shirt/tie or smart trousers/shoes); driver support line 020 7107 0711 (2022 guide). — [Splend – How to become a driver for Green Tomato Cars (2022)](https://www.splend.com/en-GB/blog/how-to-become-a-driver-for-green-tomato-cars-in-london-complete-2022-guide)
- Green Tomato says drivers "undertake rigorous driver training and only the best 25% get the opportunity to drive for us." — [Green Tomato Cars – About](https://www.greentomatocars.com/about/)
- Owned by Addison Lee since July 2023 (Travelhire acquisition). — [Wikipedia – Green Tomato Cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Tomato_Cars)

### Inferences
- Since the 2023 takeover, Green Tomato driver recruitment may now run through Addison Lee's processes and vehicle lists. A low-emission PHEV like the Superb iV fits Green Tomato's eco brand better than Addison Lee's premium list, so it is worth asking.

### Gaps
- Current (2025–2026) owner-driver fees, vehicle list, and whether the scheme still exists post-acquisition: not found.

## Q3. Wheely and Blacklane (app-based chauffeur platforms using owner-driver partners)

### Takeaway
Both work with owner-operators, but neither is a fit for a Superb: Wheely Business Class takes only Mercedes E-Class/EQE/EQS or BMW 5/i5 (and possibly 7/i7) up to 3 years old. Blacklane only partners with insured, established chauffeur companies and sets city-specific model lists, which don't name the Superb.

### Cited Findings
- Wheely: chauffeurs/partners buy and maintain their own vehicles; partner may be a sole trader (UTR) or company. — [Business Traveller](https://www.businesstraveller.com/features/chauffeur-services-take-a-back-seat/); [Wheely – Required documents](https://wheely.com/en/faq-for-drivers/uk/2424649/4191346)
- Wheely Business accreditation: BMW 5 Series (G60)/i5 (G60), Mercedes-Benz E-Class, EQS or EQE, not older than 3 years; not all colours accepted. — [Wheely – Become a Chauffeur](https://wheely.com/partner)
- Older FAQ versions also list BMW 7/i7 (G70) and colour/trim rules (black exterior for some lines; set glossy colours); E-Class W214 and BMW 7 internal camera must be covered with matte vinyl. Versions conflict. — [Wheely – Vehicle Requirements](https://wheely.com/en/faq-for-drivers/uk/7104480/8683384); [Wheely – Vehicle Standards](https://wheely.com/en/faq-for-drivers/uk/2424650/4197375)
- Wheely process: register and upload documents (select TfL as licensing authority), e-learning, accreditation at Wheely Academy; can be driving within ~3 days. — [Wheely – Becoming a Wheely Chauffeur](https://wheely.com/faq-for-drivers/uk/2424649/4191334)
- Blacklane only partners with insured, pre-existing chauffeur companies; chauffeurs generally need 1+ year professional chauffeur experience in their city; minimum age 21–25. — [Blacklane partner help – Can anyone become a partner](https://partner-help.blacklane.com/en/articles/8420689-can-anyone-become-a-blacklane-partner)
- Blacklane accepted vehicles vary by city and are shared at the start of the application. — [Blacklane help – Which vehicles](https://help.blacklane.com/en/articles/8420785-which-vehicles-can-i-use-to-work-with-blacklane); [Blacklane – become a driver](https://www.blacklane.com/en/blog/chauffeurs/become-driver-blacklane/)

### Inferences
- A Skoda Superb Estate iV does not meet Wheely's published Business list, so it is not eligible.
- For Blacklane, the driver would need to set up as (or join) an insured chauffeur company with operator-level insurance. Blacklane's London Business Class list is not published in the sources found, and Superb eligibility is unconfirmed. Blacklane rides are pre-booked (offers can be accepted ahead), which fits the driver's goals if eligible.

### Gaps
- Wheely and Blacklane commission rates: not found in this research.
- Blacklane London vehicle list: not public in sources found.

## Q4. Veezu, Bolt (incl. Bolt Business), Gett/ComCab, Dial-a-Cab, Kabbee

### Takeaway
None of these is a clear owner-driver account-work route for a Superb in London. Veezu is mainly a regional (non-London) operator, with no London own-car fee found. Bolt does let drivers see and accept scheduled (pre-booked) rides in the driver app, which is the closest "accept jobs ahead" option among apps. Gett and ComCab are black-cab only. Kabbee is a booking aggregator for minicab firms, not a direct driver recruiter.

### Cited Findings
- Veezu offers weekly PHV rental and weekly-paid insurance (exclusive to Veezu driver-partners) and advertises "ultra-low commission", with no % stated. No London-specific own-car fee found. — [Veezu – Drive](https://www.veezu.co.uk/drive); [Veezu – Hire a vehicle](https://veezu.co.uk/drive/products/hire-a-vehicle); [Veezu – Insure](https://www.veezu.co.uk/drive/products/insure)
- Bolt London scheduled rides: drivers can see all scheduled ride requests in the Bolt Driver app; price locked on acceptance; driver waits up to 5 min free; riders cancel free up to 1 hr before. — [Taxi Point – Bolt scheduling](https://www.taxi-point.co.uk/post/bolt-introduces-advanced-taxi-and-phv-scheduling-for-londoners); [ProDriver – Bolt scheduled rides](https://www.prodrivermags.com/news/bolt-launches-scheduled-ride-service-for-london-customers/)
- Bolt's June 2026 UK driver newsletter says drivers can accept trips up to 7 days in advance; the rider feature page says up to 90 days. Sources conflict. — [Bolt UK driver newsletter June 2026](https://sites.google.com/bolt.eu/bolt-uk-driver-newsletter/june-2026); [Bolt – Scheduled rides](https://bolt.eu/en/rides/features/scheduled-rides/)
- Scheduled-ride access requires a minimum number of completed Bolt rides, recent activity in the pickup area, and no history of cancelling scheduled rides. — [Bolt support – Feature access requirements](https://bolt.eu/en-lt/support/articles/81554/)
- Gett London drivers are black-cab (Knowledge) drivers; Gett's PHV option is fulfilled by Freenow drivers, and Gett drivers are not expected to do PHV bookings. — [Taxi Point – Gett private hire option](https://www.taxi-point.co.uk/post/gett-introduces-private-hire-option-as-freenow-integration-moves-closer); [Gett London](https://gett.com/uk/london/)
- ComCab (black-cab dispatcher, owned by Addison Lee) renewed its TfL Dial-a-Ride contract from 1 Sept 2026. It may send PHVs from TfL-licensed PHV operators when no taxi is available. — [Taxi Point – ComCab Dial-a-Ride contract](https://www.taxi-point.co.uk/post/comcab-extends-17-year-tfl-partnership-with-major-new-five-year-dial-a-ride-taxi-contract)
- Kabbee is a comparison/booking platform for accredited licensed London minicab firms (70+ fleets in 2014), not a direct employer of drivers. Sources are 2011–2015. — [The Next Web (2011)](https://thenextweb.com/news/kabbee-is-like-expedia-for-london-minicabs); [Fleet News (2014)](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/2014/11/12/kabbee-cuts-dead-mileage-by-half/54056/)

### Inferences
- Bolt is the most practical "see and accept pre-booked jobs ahead" option on an app the driver can join with own car/insurance and no weekly fee (commission-based). Bolt Business rides come through the same driver app, but this was not separately confirmed.
- Dial-a-Cab: black-cab co-operative, so not applicable to a PHV (no reliable 2025–2026 source found; treat as background knowledge).

### Gaps
- Veezu London presence and own-car fees: not confirmed (site fetch blocked).
- Bolt commission % for London and Bolt Business specifics: not researched here.
- Kabbee's current (2025–2026) status: not found.
- Xeta, Pink Ladies: no results found in this research.

## Q6. Local minicab/airport firms actively recruiting owner-drivers (Heathrow/West London and other boroughs)

### Takeaway
Local firms commonly offer either a fixed weekly "rent" (circuit fee) or commission. Examples found: Greyhound Cars £152/week or 20–50% commission; Two47 23% commission; Airport Transfer Cars 23% commission (+20% if using their car). Estates are explicitly sought for airport work. Vehicle age rules are looser (e.g., Swift Cars accepts 2015+). The specific firms named in the brief (Mornington, Lewisham Cars, Abbey Cars, Mount Cars) returned no current recruitment terms.

### Cited Findings
- Greyhound Cars Ltd (South London): rent £152/week with open shift, or commission: 20% for >10 jobs/week, 50% for <10 jobs/week; driver's choice. — [Indeed – PCO driver jobs Heathrow (listing)](https://uk.indeed.com/q-pco-driver-l-heathrow-jobs.html)
- Swift Cars: Heathrow–central London is its most common journey; vehicle no older than 2015 reg, PCO licence, 3+ months London minicab experience; claims saloon drivers make £1,500–£4,000/week (company claim). — [Swift Cars – minicab driver jobs](https://swiftcars.co.uk/minicab-driver-jobs/)
- MiniCab Drivers Wanted: recruiting owner-drivers with late-model MPVs, executive saloons and **estates**, commission-based airport runs. — [minicab-drivers.co.uk](https://minicab-drivers.co.uk/)
- Wembley Minicabs advertises Heathrow PCO driver jobs at £1,500–£2,200/week standard (company claim). — [Wembley Minicabs – Heathrow jobs](https://wembleyminicabs.co.uk/drivers/jobs/heathrow)
- Two47 (own car): "our standard commission is 23%"; can provide a vehicle if needed. — [Two47](https://two47at.onrender.com/)
- Airport Transfer Cars Ltd: 23% commission from job earnings + 20% for car rental (43% total if using their car); a fixed weekly rent option also exists, and drivers may work for other companies. — [JobToday – PCO driver listing](https://jobtoday.com/gb/job/pco-driver-9wlGgx)
- Minicab Lane publishes a driver payment policy (airport transfers). — [Minicab Lane – driver payment policy](https://minicablane.com/driver-payment-policy)
- Running costs (fuel, insurance, maintenance, commission, depreciation, idle time) materially reduce gross fares. — [2 Friends Minicabs blog](https://2friendsminicabs.com/blog/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-mini-cab-driver-in-london)

### Inferences
- For a driver who wants no weekly fee, commission-only local firms (about 20–25%) are the closest fit. A 2023 Superb Estate PHEV easily meets typical local age rules and is well suited to estate/large-luggage airport jobs. Commission-only terms sometimes carry minimum job thresholds (e.g., Greyhound's 50% rate below 10 jobs/week), which penalise part-time top-up drivers.
- Most local firms use Autocab/iCabbi-type dispatch apps where drivers can see pre-booked jobs. This was not confirmed per firm in this research.

### Gaps
- Mornington Cars, Lewisham Cars, Abbey Cars, Mount Cars: no 2025–2026 recruitment terms found.
- Ryde/Autocab network driver terms: not researched (no results).
- Whether these local firms accept the driver's own annual H&R policy (vs. requiring operator fleet policy): not stated in listings found.
- Independent driver reviews (UKPHD, Reddit, Trustpilot) on job volume 2025–2026 for local firms: not found in this research.
