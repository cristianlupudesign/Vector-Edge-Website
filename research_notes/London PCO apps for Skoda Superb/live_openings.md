### Inferences
- The best fit for this driver's preferences is A1 (Gumtree West Ealing, 77%/23%, no stated weekly fee, pre-booked airport, no obligation to accept jobs). It is commission-only, fits a flexible schedule, and is the only dated recent ad. The advertiser and their TfL operator licence must be confirmed before signing up. Check the TfL public register of licensed operators.
- Addison Lee's own-car route has a **colour rule (black/dark grey)** and a **5-year age limit**. A black 2023 Superb should comply until about 2028, provided the model is accepted; confirm with recruitment. Just Airports' 4-year limit would expire around 2027.
- Several "no commission / £200–£400 per day / £1,500–£2,500 per week" ads are marketing claims from agency-style sites. Some of these mention deposits. Treat them cautiously.
- Blacklane needs a limited company (one-person is fine). The driver would need to incorporate or join an existing Blacklane partner.
